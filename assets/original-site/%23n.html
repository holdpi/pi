TRANSACTION_TAX

Every buy and sell is taxed at 7%. The fee splits two ways: 85% to holders, 15% to liquidity / market support.

(02.2)

REDISTRIBUTION

Tax accumulates in a pool. Every 1h it airdrops to holders by balance × time-multiplier. Multiplier resets on buy. Hard cap: ×2.5.

SELL_TAX_DECAY

Selling early is penalized. Patience reduces your exit cost. Tax decays smoothly: 3% + 7% · e−t (t in days).

(02.4)

MARKET_SUPPORT

15% of every tax funds the liquidity engine: depth, slippage smoothing, basic market-making logic.

(06) — FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

// click to expand.

  • What is the contract address? +
    The live contract address is posted at the top of the page (and again in the footer). Always verify on the official site or Twitter — never trust addresses from DMs or random Telegram messages.
  • How often does redistribution happen? +
    Every hour, automatically. The accumulated tax pool airdrops to all holders, weighted by balance × time-multiplier. Multiplier scales from ×1.0 at day 0 to a hard cap of ×2.5 long-term. No manual claim required.
  • How is the 7% tax split? +
    85% goes to the redistribution pool (airdropped to holders every hour). 15% goes to liquidity / market support — depth, slippage smoothing, basic market-making logic.
  • How does the sell tax work? +
    Sell tax decays smoothly with hold time using SellTax(t) = 3% + 7% · e−t (t in days since your last buy). Instant sell = 10%. After 1 day: ~5.6%. After 3 days: ~3.3%. Long-term floor: 3%. Selling early is penalized; patience reduces your exit cost.
  • Does buying reset my multiplier? +
    Yes. Any increase in your position resets your hold-time clock to zero. The multiplier is computed as 1 + 1.5 · t / (t + 2) with t in days — fast growth at first, then asymptotes to the ×2.5 hard cap.
  • Why an hourly cycle? +
    Hourly drops keep the system feeling alive — 24 events per day means rewards land while you're awake, not just once or twice. Solana's low fees make per-hour distribution viable without dust gas costs.
  • Is the contract audited? +
    Audit is in progress (see Roadmap → PUBLIC_AUDIT). Full report will be published openly when complete. Until then: only commit what you're comfortable risking.
  • What happens if trading volume dies? +
    Distributions slow down or stop. The mechanic is volume-driven by design — no volume = no tax = no pool. We don't pretend otherwise. See the Disclosure section below.
  • Is there a team allocation? +
    No. Fair launch — 100% of supply enters the public market at launch. No presale, no team wallet, no insider unlocks. The contract will be renounced post-launch.
